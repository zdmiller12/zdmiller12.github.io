A beautiful male Northern Cardinal photographed during a snowy January morning. Cardinals are year-round residents in Ontario and their brilliant red plumage provides a striking contrast against the white winter landscape.

This photograph was taken from my kitchen window while the cardinal was feeding at a sunflower seed feeder. The early morning light created perfect illumination, highlighting the bird's distinctive crest and vibrant coloring.

**Behavior Notes:**
- Cardinals are non-migratory and well-adapted to cold climates
- Males are more brightly colored than females
- They typically feed in early morning and late afternoon
- Known for their distinctive "birdy-birdy-birdy" call
